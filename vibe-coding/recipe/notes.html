Bills Barn Buffalo Wings

Recipe Notes, Memories & Game Day Tips

About This Recipe

These Bills Barn Buffalo Wings are made for game days, family gatherings, and any time we want crispy wings with plenty of Buffalo flavor without making them overly hot.

The key to the extra-crispy texture is drying the wings thoroughly before cooking and using baking powder in the seasoning mixture. Giving the wings time to rest uncovered in the refrigerator can make them even crispier.

The mild Buffalo sauce combines hot sauce, butter, honey, Worcestershire sauce, and garlic powder. The result is a buttery, tangy sauce with just enough heat to taste like classic Buffalo wings while still being mild enough for a crowd.
